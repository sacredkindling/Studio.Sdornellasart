On the work

My name is Stephen D'Ornellas and I am a Guyanese-born Canadian artist. My work has been shown and sold to collectors in Canada, the United States, London and Guyana.

Alongside my own body of work, I collaborate with clients who commission paintings based on the style of pieces I've already made — adapting scale, palette, or subject to fit a particular space or request.
